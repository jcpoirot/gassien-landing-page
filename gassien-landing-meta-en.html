5 simple, quick steps

From measuring to decorating, every step is designed to be quick and simple. You stay in control from the first measurement to the last object in place.

Like a construction set: wall bases, shelves and accessories to combine freely.

  1. 1

    Step 1: Measure your wall

    A tape measure is all you need: note the height and width to cover. Large wall, nook or brick wall, anything goes.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Design online

    Place your pieces in the configurator, try, move, start again, until you find the design that suits you.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Receive your kit

    Order in a few clicks: everything is delivered straight to your door.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Install with ease

    Fix the bases to the wall, then place the shelves and accessories wherever you like. No DIY expertise needed.

  5. 5

    Step 5: Decorate and enjoy

    Books, plants, objects: your wall comes to life. And tomorrow, your design evolves with you.
